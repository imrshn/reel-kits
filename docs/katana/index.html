The style brief

Change any part of it: the colours, the words, the ending

Create a kinetic typography promo using the supplied reference images and brand information. Follow the reference's rapid sequence of oversized condensed headlines, stacked words, isolated punctuation, and small supporting text. Alternate near-black backgrounds with warm off-white and vivid red panels. Animate lettering through punchy scale changes, lateral sweeps, stretching, rotation, and motion blur. Interleave framed interface or reference-image inserts, simple red geometric forms, thin glowing lines, and subtle particles. Keep the composition bold and tightly timed, then resolve into a clean centered brand end card.

Step by step

The setup

  1. 1
    Get a paid Higgsfield planKatana runs on Higgsfield credits and doesn't work on a free account. Sign up at higgsfield.ai.
  2. 2
    Add Higgsfield to ClaudeIn Claude, go to Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector. Name it Higgsfield, paste https://mcp.higgsfield.ai/mcp, then sign in to Higgsfield. No API key needed.
  3. 3
    Start a new chat and send prompt 1It runs the kinetic typography preset. Claude asks you to upload what goes in the video: your product, logo, characters, clothes or props (up to 5 photos).
  4. 4
    Upload your images, then send prompt 2That's the style brief. Change the colours, words or brand to fit yours.
  5. 5
    Check the credit cost and confirmClaude shows the cost on a card before anything is made. Your video appears in the chat with a download button and is saved to Assets on higgsfield.ai.
  6. 6
    Ask for changes in the same chatUse the tweak lines below. Timing and cut fixes don't use credits.
