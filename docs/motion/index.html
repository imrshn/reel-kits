Prompt

Build the kinetic-type animation

Gives you one HTML file. Open it in your browser and click to play.

Build me a kinetic-typography motion-graphics piece as ONE self-contained HTML file. No libraries, no images, no build step: just HTML, CSS and JavaScript in a single file that I open in a browser and play full screen. It must look like a premium, bright, futuristic launch piece, not a slideshow.

## Look
- Dark "neon signal" style: near-black background (#050508), electric blue (#2D6BFF) and hot magenta (#FF2D8A) as the only colours, white type. A faint blue grid that drifts, two slow neon rings, blue and magenta halos, drifting glowing dots, a slow light sweep, fine scanlines and film grain over everything.
- Every big word gets a chromatic split: a blue copy and a magenta copy of each letter sit behind the white letter and fly apart on impact, then settle 3 px either side. Big words glow (blue or magenta text shadow).
- Fonts from Google Fonts: Anybody (a variable font with a width axis from 50 to 150, so letters can really stretch, not just scale), JetBrains Mono for HUD readouts and code, Instrument Serif italic for the small connective words.
- Chips are outlined neon pills; strike-throughs are magenta bars that slash in with a glow. Panels are dark glass with a blue border.
- The ONE contrast moment is inverted: at "That cut" the frame snaps to white with black type, holds through "All of it," then snaps back to black on "code".
- Hits: on every slam a 100 ms white flash over the frame, and a shock ring that expands and fades. A glitch burst (coloured scan bands jumping) on "cut" and on "code".
- Stage is 1920x1080 and scales to fit any window. Never slow: something moves every frame, the picture changes every 2 to 4 seconds, and the camera drifts, pushes in, whips sideways with motion blur and pulls back. A running timecode sits top left.

## How it plays
- A click or the space bar starts it (browsers block autoplay). URL `?auto=1` skips the gate. `?t=12` jumps to 12 seconds and holds that frame. Expose `window.__seek(seconds)` so a script can render frames deterministically.
- At time 0 show a 100 ms white flash (a sync marker for editing), then the piece starts at PRE = 0.8 s.
- If a file named `vo.mp3` sits next to the HTML, play it from PRE; otherwise play silently.
- Use the Web Animations API with a single timeline so seeking is exact. GPU-friendly properties only (transform, opacity, clip-path), 60 fps on a laptop.

## The words
At the top of the file put an editable JS block: `LINES` (the spoken lines) and `WORDS` (each word with start and end seconds, measured from my voice recording). Every spoken word must appear on screen at its start time as big kinetic type. If someone edits the words and has no timings, spread the words evenly across each line's duration. The words and timings are below.
