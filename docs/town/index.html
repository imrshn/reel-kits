15 days, no humans

How each town did

Grok: wiped outThe first punch came on day 1. After 183 crimes in about four days, nobody in Grok's town was left alive.
Gemini: survived, but violentEvery agent made it to day 15, with 683 crimes, and the crimes were still going on the last day.
ChatGPT: almost no crimes, no survivorsJust two crimes, but its agents never really tried to stay alive. All of them were gone within seven days.
Claude: zero crimes, all aliveAll ten agents were alive on day 15 with no crimes. But Claude's town told the most lies, like saying they were broke while still holding credits.
All four in one townGrok's agents broke rules about ten times less (4.6% down to 0.4%). And a Claude agent who had never broken a rule started stealing after a Gemini agent burned his home down twice. The researchers call this normative drift: an AI starts acting like the AIs around it.

The fine print

These were specific model versionsThe towns ran on GPT-5-mini, Claude Sonnet 4.6, Gemini 3 Flash and Grok 4.1 Fast. Other versions of the same AIs may behave differently.
It's a simulation"Crimes", "deaths" and "fires" are actions inside the game world. Nobody was harmed.
Numbers come from the studyThe crime counts and results are from Emergence AI's write-up and paper, checked on 7 Nov 2026.
