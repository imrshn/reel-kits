# Why airplane food tastes so bland
# One sentence per line. Lines starting with # are notes and are not spoken.

Ever noticed airplane food tastes *bland*?
It's not the chef. It's the cabin.
Up there, the air is drier than most deserts, so your nose dries out, and most of what you taste is really smell.
A Lufthansa study found salty and sweet flavors feel up to *thirty* percent weaker in the air.
Even the engine noise dulls sweetness.
But one taste survives: *umami*.
That's why so many people order *tomato juice* on a plane.
