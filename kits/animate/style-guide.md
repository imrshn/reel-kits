# My reel style

Claude follows this on every reel. Say "always do X" or "save that as my style" and Claude updates it.

## Look
- Sky Hologram: bright sky-to-sunrise background, HUD corner brackets, holo chips, orbit rings.
- Fonts: Instrument Serif headlines with one gold/blue italic accent word, Plus Jakarta Sans for labels and captions.
- One dark (night) scene at the twist, for contrast.
- A new picture every 2 to 4 seconds. Things pop in on the spoken word.

## Voice and script
- Hook names the topic in the first 5 words and asks a question.
- 60 to 160 words, short sentences, plain words.
- Ends with "Follow for more ..." unless I give a comment keyword.
- Voice: free Kokoro voice `am_michael` at speed 1.12 (change in settings.json).

## Sound
- A soft sound on every cut and pop. No music unless I add a track to assets/music/.

## Captions
- Up to 3 words at a time, the spoken word in blue, accent words in gold italic.
